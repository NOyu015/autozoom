## What is AutoZoom?

AutoZoom is a browser screen recorder that auto-zooms following your clicks.

You record a tutorial, a product demo, or a bug report. While you record, AutoZoom tracks your click positions. On export, it renders a smooth video that pans and zooms toward each click and back out — so viewers can actually see what you clicked, without you adding zoom keyframes by hand in an editor.
