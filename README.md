## What is AutoZoom?

AutoZoom is a **browser screen recorder** with **automatic zoom**. It is built for **tutorial videos, product demos, online courses, bug reports and software walkthroughs**.

You record your screen in a browser tab. While you record, AutoZoom tracks your clicks. On export, it renders a smooth video that pans and zooms toward each click and back out — so viewers can actually see what you clicked, without you adding zoom keyframes by hand in a video editor.
